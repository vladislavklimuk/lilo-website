A simple and beautiful way to keep track of the places you love.

Lilo helps you save locations that matter to you and organize them into clean, structured collections — whether it's new cafés to visit, travel finds, or spots friends recommended.

Designed for clarity and privacy, Lilo keeps your lists easy to browse and simple to share when needed.
